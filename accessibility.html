Our commitment

The RK Hospitality Studio website has been designed with accessibility, readability and keyboard use in mind.

The aim is to make the website understandable, navigable and usable across a range of devices, browsers and assistive technologies.

Accessibility is treated as an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time feature.

Accessibility features

The website currently includes or aims to include:

  • Clear heading structures and page landmarks
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators
  • A skip link allowing users to move directly to the main page content
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Form labels connected to their relevant fields
  • Descriptive button and link text
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Colour contrast considered throughout the design
  • Reduced animation where a user has enabled a reduced-motion preference

Known limitations

Some third-party services, embedded content or external websites linked from this website may not meet the same accessibility standards.

Examples may include external booking platforms, mapping services, form-processing services or other websites outside the control of RK Hospitality Studio.

Portfolio screenshots and visual demonstrations may contain interface text that is not intended to replace the accessible written description provided alongside them.

Feedback and assistance

Please get in touch when you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website or require information in a different format.

Include the page address, the nature of the problem and the device or browser being used where possible.

Email: hello@rkhospitality.studio

Reasonable efforts will be made to investigate the issue and provide an accessible alternative where practical.

Ongoing improvements

The website will continue to be reviewed as content, pages and services are added.

Identified accessibility issues will be prioritised according to their effect on users and the practical work required to resolve them.
