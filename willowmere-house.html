Willowmere House

A complete booking-focused website concept for a fictional independent country hotel in Kent.

Project overview

Presenting the property through a clearer digital experience

Willowmere House was created to demonstrate how an independent hotel can communicate its character while helping guests find the practical information they need.

The project combines room presentation, guest information, destination content and a clear availability-enquiry route within one consistent visual system.

The brief

Create a complete hotel website built around guest decisions

01

Establish a clear visual identity suited to an independent country hotel.

02

Make room types easier to understand and compare.

03

Present guest information without overwhelming the user.

04

Create an enquiry journey that remains visible throughout the website.

05

Ensure the experience remains clear across desktop, tablet and mobile.

Design challenges

Balancing atmosphere with practical information

Hospitality websites need to create emotional interest while still helping guests make confident decisions.

01

Room comparison

Each room needed a consistent structure without making every option feel identical.

02

Information hierarchy

Important guest details needed to remain easy to find without dominating the homepage.

03

Booking visibility

Availability and enquiry actions needed to appear at natural decision points throughout the site.

Guest journey

A clear route from first impression to enquiry

The website was structured around the main stages a guest moves through before deciding to contact the property.

01

Discover

Understand the property, setting and reasons to stay.

02

Compare

Explore room options, facilities and occupancy.

03

Prepare

Find parking, check-in, policies and local information.

04

Enquire

Move into a clear availability or contact journey.

Website structure

Six focused pages with distinct roles

Home

Property overview, room introduction, location and main calls to action.

Rooms

Consistent accommodation comparison and room details.

Guest Information

Arrival, parking, check-in, policies and practical details.

Explore

Destination content and local reasons to stay.

About

Hotel identity, atmosphere and positioning.

Contact

Availability enquiry and direct contact information.

Homepage showcase

Establishing the property quickly

The homepage introduces the hotel through a clear headline, atmospheric imagery and immediate routes towards rooms and availability.

Supporting sections explain the property, room options, countryside setting and guest experience without forcing visitors through long blocks of text.

Room presentation

Helping guests compare accommodation confidently

Each room follows a consistent format covering occupancy, bed type, key facilities, pricing guidance and availability.

This reduces the effort required to compare options while allowing each room to retain its own visual identity.

Guest information strategy

Answering practical questions before they become barriers

Parking, check-in, accessibility, breakfast and policies are presented in clearly separated sections.

This supports guest confidence and reduces the need to search across multiple pages or contact the hotel for basic information.

Destination content

Selling the wider stay, not only the room

The Explore page connects the hotel with the surrounding Kent countryside, nearby towns and local attractions.

This gives guests more reasons to choose the property and supports longer, experience-led stays.

Direct-enquiry journey

Keeping the next step visible

Availability and contact actions appear throughout the website at the points where guests are most likely to need them.

The final contact page provides a focused enquiry form, contact information and supporting reassurance without unnecessary distractions.

Responsive experience

Designed for desktop, tablet and mobile guests

The layouts adapt across screen sizes while preserving the same hierarchy, content and enquiry journey.

Collapsible navigation

A focused mobile menu keeps every key page accessible.

Responsive typography

Heading and body sizes adapt without losing hierarchy.

Stacked layouts

Multi-column content becomes clear single-column sections.

Touch-friendly actions

Buttons and form fields remain comfortable to use.
