Privacy Policy

This policy explains how RK Hospitality Studio collects, uses, stores and protects personal information.

Who is responsible for your information?

RK Hospitality Studio is responsible for deciding how and why personal information submitted through this website is used.

Contact email: hello@rkhospitality.studio

Information that may be collected

Depending on how you use the website, the following information may be collected:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number where supplied
  • Your role or position
  • Your business or property name
  • Your website address
  • Information about your website, business, requirements, budget or project
  • Messages and other information included in an enquiry
  • Technical information associated with a form submission, such as the submission time, browser information and IP-related security data where processed by the form provider
  • Cookie and analytics information where applicable and permitted

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for the enquiry.

How information is used

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to general enquiries
  • Provide a requested website review
  • Assess and discuss a potential project
  • Prepare proposals, quotations and contracts
  • Deliver and manage agreed services
  • Communicate with clients during a project
  • Issue invoices and maintain accounting records
  • Handle questions, concerns or complaints
  • Maintain the security and operation of the website
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary

Information will not be sold to third parties.

Lawful bases for using information

The lawful basis used will depend on the purpose and circumstances.

Legitimate interests

Legitimate interests may be relied upon to respond to enquiries, provide requested website reviews, discuss services, manage ordinary business communications, prevent misuse and protect the website.

These interests are considered against the rights and reasonable expectations of the person concerned.

Steps before entering a contract

Information may be used when you ask RK Hospitality Studio to consider a project, provide a quotation or take steps before entering into an agreement.

Contract

Where services are agreed, information may be needed to perform and manage the contract.

Legal obligation

Certain information may need to be retained or used to comply with accounting, taxation or other legal requirements.

Consent

Consent may be used for optional cookies, analytics or marketing where required. Consent can be withdrawn, although withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful use.

Sharing information

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers supporting the website or business.

Formspree

The contact form and complimentary website-review form are processed using Formspree.

When you submit either form, the information entered is transmitted to Formspree so that the submission can be processed, protected against misuse, stored within the relevant form inbox and delivered to RK Hospitality Studio by email.

Formspree may process technical information connected with a submission for security, spam prevention and service operation.

Other information may be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • Website and domain-hosting providers
  • Business email providers
  • Cloud-storage or business-software providers
  • Payment, invoicing or accounting providers
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary
  • Public authorities where disclosure is legally required

Service providers are expected to handle information securely and only for the relevant purpose.

International transfers

Some technology providers used by RK Hospitality Studio may process or store personal information outside the United Kingdom.

This may include information submitted through forms processed by Formspree.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, the relevant provider is expected to use an appropriate legal transfer mechanism or safeguard under applicable data-protection law.

How long information is kept

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal requirements and the handling of disputes.

Information Typical retention period
Contact-form enquiries

Up to 12 months after the last meaningful communication, unless the enquiry becomes part of a client project or legal record

Website-review requests

Up to 12 months after the review, completion of correspondence or last meaningful contact

Formspree submission records

Removed from the form inbox when no longer needed for the enquiry, review, security or record-keeping purpose

Unsuccessful proposals

Up to 12 months after the proposal closes

Active project information

For the duration of the project

Client project files

Up to six years after project completion

Contracts, invoices and accounts

Up to six years after the end of the relevant tax year

Complaints and disputes

Up to six years after closure, or longer where reasonably required

Cookie preferences

According to the duration disclosed by the relevant consent mechanism

Information may be deleted earlier when it is no longer required, or retained longer where there is a genuine legal or operational reason.

Information security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

These measures may include secure hosting, encrypted connections, access controls, account security, backups and limiting access to those who need the information.

No internet transmission or electronic-storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your information rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Ask for information to be deleted
  • Ask for use of information to be restricted
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Receive certain information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where consent is relied upon

Right to object

You have the right to object where personal information is used on the basis of legitimate interests. Any objection will be considered in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

To make a request, email hello@rkhospitality.studio .

Identity may need to be confirmed before information is disclosed or changed.

Cookies and analytics

The completed website may use strictly necessary technology required for security, forms or saved preferences.

Optional analytics or similar technology will not be activated without an appropriate choice where consent is required.

A separate cookie notice or preference control will be added if non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are introduced.

Questions and complaints

Questions about this policy or the use of personal information should first be sent to:

hello@rkhospitality.studio

You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK supervisory authority for data protection.

This policy may be updated when the website, services, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top of the page will show when it was last reviewed.
