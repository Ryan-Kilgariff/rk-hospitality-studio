What information we collect

Depending on how you interact with RK Hospitality Studio, we may collect or use:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number where supplied
  • Your business or property name
  • Your role within a business where supplied
  • Your business or venue location
  • Your website address
  • Project requirements
  • Website-review information
  • Booking-system information
  • Website content supplied for a project
  • Project and enquiry correspondence
  • Records of meetings, discussions and decisions

Where you become a client, we may also need to use information connected with:

  • Contracts and service history
  • Invoices and payments
  • Financial transaction records
  • Complaints, disputes or legal claims
  • Other information reasonably required to comply with legal or accounting obligations

We do not intentionally request special-category personal information through this website. Please do not include sensitive personal information in an enquiry unless it is genuinely necessary.

How we use personal information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to business enquiries
  • Provide requested website reviews
  • Assess prospective projects
  • Prepare quotations and proposals
  • Enter into and manage client contracts
  • Deliver website and related services
  • Communicate during a project
  • Manage ordinary business administration
  • Issue invoices and maintain accounting records
  • Respond to queries and complaints
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary
  • Protect the operation and security of the website

Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Lawful bases and data-protection rights

UK data-protection law requires a lawful basis for collecting and using personal information. The basis used depends on the reason the information is being processed.

Contract

We may process personal information where it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform a contract with you.

This may include discussing a project, preparing a quotation, delivering agreed services and managing the client relationship.

Legitimate interests

RK Hospitality Studio has a legitimate interest in using limited personal information to respond to business enquiries, assess prospective projects, prepare website reviews and proposals, communicate with potential clients and manage ordinary business administration.

This information is limited to what is reasonably necessary, such as names, business contact details, business names, website addresses, project requirements and relevant correspondence.

People who submit an enquiry or request a website review would reasonably expect this information to be used so that RK Hospitality Studio can understand and respond to their request.

This processing benefits both the person making the request and RK Hospitality Studio by allowing enquiries to be handled efficiently, appropriate services to be considered and accurate information to be provided.

We also have legitimate interests in using relevant information to investigate and resolve complaints, maintain accurate records, protect legal and commercial interests and establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

Information used for these purposes is limited to what is relevant to the matter and is not used for unrelated purposes.

Legal obligation

We may process and retain personal information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, including accounting, taxation and other applicable requirements.

Consent

Where consent is relied upon for a particular activity, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place lawfully before consent was withdrawn.

Where we get personal information from

Personal information may be obtained:

  • Directly from you
  • From publicly available business sources, such as business websites and public business profiles

Publicly available information may be reviewed when researching prospective hospitality businesses or assessing a website that has been submitted for review.

Who we share information with

We use a limited number of technology providers to operate the website and communicate with prospective and existing clients.

GitHub Pages

The RK Hospitality Studio website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

GitHub may process technical information required to deliver, secure and operate the website, including information associated with requests made to the website.

Formspree

The contact form and complimentary website-review form are processed using Formspree.

When you submit either form, the information entered is transmitted to Formspree so the submission can be processed, protected against misuse, stored in the relevant form inbox and delivered to RK Hospitality Studio.

Formspree may also process technical information connected with a submission for security, spam prevention and service operation.

Google Workspace

Google Workspace is used for business email and related cloud services.

Personal information contained in emails, enquiries, attachments and business correspondence may be processed so that RK Hospitality Studio can communicate with prospective and existing clients and manage relevant business records.

Other recipients

Information may also be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • Professional or legal advisers
  • Accounting or financial-service providers
  • Relevant regulatory authorities
  • Organisations with which we are legally required to share information

Sharing information outside the UK

Some technology providers used by RK Hospitality Studio may process or store personal information outside the United Kingdom.

This may include information processed through GitHub, Formspree or Google Workspace.

Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, the relevant provider is expected to use an appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguard in accordance with UK data-protection law.

Google Workspace may process information in the United States and other locations in accordance with its applicable international data-transfer arrangements.

How long we keep personal information

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, applicable legal requirements and the handling of disputes.

Information Typical retention period
General enquiries

Up to 12 months after the last meaningful communication

Website-review requests

Up to 12 months after completion of the review or last meaningful communication

Unsuccessful proposals

Up to 12 months after the proposal or prospective project closes

Active project information

For the duration of the project and as reasonably necessary to manage the service

Client project files

Up to six years after project completion

Contracts, invoices and accounts

Up to six years after the end of the relevant tax year

Complaints and disputes

Up to six years after closure, or longer where reasonably required for an ongoing legal matter

Formspree submission records

Removed from the form inbox when no longer required for the enquiry, website review, security or relevant record-keeping purpose

Information may be deleted earlier where it is no longer required, or kept for longer where there is a genuine legal or regulatory reason to do so.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have the following rights.

Right of access

You may ask for copies of personal information we hold about you and information about how it is used.

Right to rectification

You may ask us to correct personal information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.

Right to erasure

In certain circumstances, you may ask us to delete your personal information.

Right to restriction

In certain circumstances, you may ask us to restrict the way your personal information is used.

Right to object

You may object to certain processing of your personal information, including processing based on legitimate interests.

Right to data portability

Where applicable, you may ask us to transfer certain personal information you provided to another organisation or directly to you.

Right to withdraw consent

Where consent is used as the lawful basis, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Making a rights request

To exercise a data-protection right, email hello@rkhospitality.studio .

We may need to confirm your identity before disclosing or changing personal information.

Information security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Measures may include encrypted connections, account security, access controls, secure technology providers and limiting access to information to situations where it is genuinely required.

No internet transmission or electronic-storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies and analytics

At launch, RK Hospitality Studio does not use Google Analytics or another non-essential behavioural analytics service on this website.

The website may use technologies that are strictly necessary for hosting, security, form processing or essential website operation.

If optional analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, this privacy notice will be updated and an appropriate cookie or consent mechanism will be implemented where required.
