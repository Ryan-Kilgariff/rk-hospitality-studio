What the review covers

Six areas that shape the online guest experience

Each area is considered from the perspective of a guest deciding whether to stay, enquire or book.

01

Mobile usability

Navigation, text sizing, image behaviour, buttons and the overall ease of using the site on smaller screens.

02

Booking journey

How clearly guests can move from browsing the website to checking availability, enquiring or booking.

03

Room presentation

The clarity and consistency of room imagery, facilities, occupancy, pricing and calls to action.

04

Guest information

The visibility of parking, check-in, breakfast, accessibility, policies and practical details.

05

Trust and confidence

How effectively the website communicates legitimacy, professionalism, consistency and reassurance.

06

Direct-booking opportunities

Areas where the website could better support direct enquiries, reservations or guest conversion.

What you receive

Practical feedback rather than a generic score

The purpose of the review is to highlight useful observations that can guide the next decision about the website.

01

What is already working

Strong elements worth preserving or developing further.

02

Main usability issues

The areas most likely to create confusion, hesitation or unnecessary effort.

03

Priority improvements

A focused list of changes that should be addressed first.

04

Suitable next step

An honest view on whether the site needs focused improvement, substantial transformation or a full redesign.
